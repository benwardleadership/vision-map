## Design Rules

- Use the exact brand colors only: teal `#1DC0DC`, navy `#072543`, yellow `#FADE4C`, steel `#B1B8BD`.
- Use off-white `#F4F8FB`, white `#FFFFFF`, body ink `#23364A`, and muted `#7A8DA0` for surfaces and text roles.
- Never use pure black. Navy is the dark type and surface color.
- Averta is used for all titles, body copy, UI, and navigation.
- Hollanda is reserved for signature/accent moments only.
- Use supplied logo and mark files as-is. Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, crop, add effects, or recreate marks.
- Keep strong contrast. Never place a teal mark on a teal background.
- Navy is for impact. Light surfaces are for clarity.
- Yellow is used sparingly for key moments, not decoration.

## Voice Rules

- Premium clarity. Never motivational hype.
- Conversational, direct, practical, confident, warm, and challenging.
- Short paragraphs. One thought at a time.
- Strong declarative openings.
- Close personal content with "Make it a great day." where appropriate.
